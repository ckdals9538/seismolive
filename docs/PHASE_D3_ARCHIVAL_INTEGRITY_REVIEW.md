## 3. Mandatory Targeted Audits & Corrections

### 3.1 2016 Kumamoto: Tectonic Graben vs. Aso Volcanic Eruption

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Scientific Verification**: The April 16, 2016 Kumamoto earthquake ($M_w\,7.0$) was fundamentally a **tectonic strike-slip faulting event** along the Futagawa-Hinagu fault zone within the Beppu-Shimabara graben. It was **NOT** a volcanic eruption of Mount Aso.

1. **Layer A (Archival Reality)**:
   - Eyewitnesses around Mashiki and Minamiaso reported extreme horizontal shearing (`割れる`) and deep subterranean groaning reverberating through the caldera mountain walls (`唸る`).
2. **Layer B (Seismological Integrity)**:
   - InSAR and field geodetic surveys confirmed up to $2 \sim 3\,\text{m}$ of right-lateral surface displacement along the Futagawa fault terminating near the western edge of the Aso caldera.
   - While secondary steam venting and localized fumarolic shifts occurred inside the caldera due to dynamic stress perturbation, the magma chamber was not ruptured, and volcanic explosive eruptions did not trigger the earthquake.
3. **Layer C (Artistic Soundfield Correction)**:
   - **STRICT PROHIBITION**: Never use synthesized volcanic lava eruptions, bubbling magma sounds, or Hollywood explosion booms.
   - **Implemented Design**: The soundfield represents graben fault shearing via a highpass-filtered pink-noise friction generator ($130\,\text{Hz}$) routed into a narrow $65\,\text{ms}$ valley slapback delay, coupled with a deep $32\,\text{Hz}$ bedrock resonance reflecting off the caldera rim.

> [!WARNING]
> **Scientific Caveat**: Sand boils and mud venting (`솟구치다`) observed in Heunghae-eup were **localized near-surface secondary phenomena** caused by elevated pore water pressure in unconsolidated Quaternary alluvium. Liquefaction was **NOT** the root fault rupture mechanism of the earthquake.

1. **Layer A (Archival Reality)**:
   - Farmers and residents observed groundwater and silt erupting several meters into the air from cracks in rice paddies immediately following the shaking (`솟구치다`).

   - Sand boils occurred exclusively in the upper $10 \sim 15\,\text{m}$ of soft alluvial sediments where cyclic S-wave shear strain liquefied saturated sand layers.
3. **Layer C (Artistic Soundfield Correction)**:
   - **STRICT PROHIBITION**: Never use cartoonish mud-splashing SFX, wet squelches, or cinematic water geyser sounds.
